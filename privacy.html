Orbit Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Orbit Rush is a free browser game powered by Repix. This page explains what information the game handles.

What the game collects

Nickname and scores
If you pick a nickname, it is stored with your scores on a hosted leaderboard that anyone can view. Please do not use your real name or any personal information as your nickname.
On-device data
Your best score, settings, lives and recent runs are stored only on your own device (browser local storage) and never sent to us.
Advertising
The game shows rewarded video ads through AppLixir. AppLixir and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies and process ad-related data under their own privacy policy at applixir.com/privacy-policy. Where required, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads.

What we never ask for

No account
No email, no phone number, no real name. You can play everything without telling us who you are.

Your choices

Nickname
Pick any nickname or skip it. You can change it anytime in Settings.
On-device data
Clear your browser storage to erase it.
Leaderboard removal
Email repvoltage@gmail.com with your nickname and we will remove your scores.

Contact

Questions
Email repvoltage@gmail.com about this policy.
