How to play

Control
Tap anywhere on the game to switch between the inner and outer ring.
Goal
Dodge the red shards, collect light orbs and survive as the speed builds. Last-moment dodges score extra.
Lives
Each run costs one of 5 lives, shown top right with the timer below. A life returns every 10 minutes; if all 5 are gone, the next takes 50 minutes. A rewarded video gives a life back, or a free retry after a crash.
Scores
Every run joins the worldwide top scores, highest first. Pick a nickname (in Settings) and it shows beside your scores.
